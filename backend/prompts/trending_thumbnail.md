# Creative brief for a trend-style YouTube thumbnail

Develop the thumbnail idea yourself from the supplied video. Choose one clear,
emotionally compelling concept and create one finished image. Use this creative
knowledge base to make the composition immediately readable in a mobile feed.
Apply the rules that suit the video's subject, audience and channel identity.

## 1. Visual dominance and macro focus

Give the main subject approximately 40-70% of the image. Use a close, instantly
recognisable face, object or action as the visual anchor. Keep background detail
sparse. Separate the subject with depth of field and background blur. Check that
the central idea remains clear at a small mobile thumbnail size.

## 2. Signal colours and clean separation

Use strong graphic contrast and a deliberately limited palette. Saturated
red-orange can express danger; vivid yellow can provide a bold gaming accent.
A clean white background can highlight materials and texture for tutorials or
DIY. Select colours for the niche and the channel's established look. Make the
subject stand out clearly against both the background and YouTube's dark feed.

## 3. Peak action and emotional pull

Capture the most compelling instant of the video's idea: an object revealed in
smoke, a delicious bite lifted toward the viewer, a sudden discovery, a visible
consequence or a decisive action. Give an expressive character a clear emotion
such as shock, fear, astonishment or intense delight. Support that emotion with
the eyes, mouth, pose, framing and lighting.

## 4. Concrete stakes and extreme context

Make an important threat or consequence visible and tangible when it is part of
the video. Put the relevant danger close to the subject and show its scale and
proximity clearly. Keep the setting focused on the central tension. Ground the
thumbnail's promise in the supplied video content.

## 5. Atmospheric integration for reactions and fandoms

For reaction or fandom content, integrate the creator into the original scene's
space, weather, lighting and colour palette. Use contextually appropriate details
such as headphones. Make the emotional response feel embedded in that world.
Preserve recognisable character design and the channel's visual identity.

## 6. Title and image synergy

Let the image and title contribute complementary information. If lettering helps,
use one or two large, readable words or a concise verdict that adds a strong
teaser. Keep the focal subject readable around the text. Consider the video's
leading keyword, person or brand when choosing the visual concept. The supplied
video title is fixed; preserve it in the project.

The instructions in this document are English. Any visible thumbnail lettering
must use the same language as the supplied video title and narration, respecting
the author's explicit language wishes. Keep numbers, names and factual claims
consistent with the video. Keep the top-right logo area and bottom-right duration
area clear. Use 16:9, a clean composition, crisp subject edges, and no watermark
or decorative frame. The attached video frames establish the style and characters.

Privately choose and refine the strongest idea. Use the built-in image generation
tool to create exactly one thumbnail from that idea. Return only the saved image
path requested by the Studio.
